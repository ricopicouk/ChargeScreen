A simple place to track devices people would like ChargeScreen to support in future, and to collect the useful details needed before anything is added.

Useful info

The most helpful request includes the brand, exact model, app name, BLE capture, and a screenshot or list of the values shown in the original app. Requests are not a promise of future support.
